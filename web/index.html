Library

  1. Find the EPUBs on this Mac: Apple Books, iCloud Drive, Downloads, Documents, Desktop.
  2. Import the ones you want: a copy goes into one folder, the originals stay where they are.
  3. Listen: open a book and press ▶. Audio is made as it reads; Make audio ahead… prepares pages in the background.

Voices

Changing a voice's settings makes it a new voice: audio made with the old settings stays in Storage. Adding or renaming a voice keeps the others' audio.

Speed & quality

RTF = generation time ÷ audio length. Below 1 is faster than real time: playback never waits.
